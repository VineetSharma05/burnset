The science

Built on physics.
Not guesswork.

Most apps give a strength session one flat number. BurnSet calculates every set from what you actually lifted.

01

Mechanical work

load × 9.81 × distance × reps ÷ 18% efficiency × 1.33

The energy to move the weight, including the share of your own bodyweight that moves (squat ≈ 85%, push-up ≈ 64%).

02

Metabolic cost

(MET − 1) × bodyweight × time under tension

What your body burns holding and stabilising during the set, using research-based MET values for every exercise.

03

Recovery burn

elevated MET × bodyweight × rest time

Your heart rate stays up between sets. BurnSet counts that too. You can switch it off.

Cardio and timed holds use the Compendium of Physical Activities. Net mode counts only calories above resting, which is the honest number for fat loss. All figures are estimates (±20%).

Questions? Answers.

How accurate are the calorie numbers?

They're physics-based estimates, typically within about ±20% of lab measurements. That's more than accurate enough to compare sets, track trends, and keep your fat-loss maths honest.

Why 7,700 calories per kilogram?

One kilogram of human body fat stores roughly 7,700 kcal of energy. Create that deficit through diet and training and you lose about a kilogram of fat. BurnSet tracks the training side precisely.

What's the difference between net and gross calories?

Gross includes the calories you would have burned anyway just being alive. Net only counts the extra calories from training, which is the number that actually creates a deficit. BurnSet uses net by default.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start logging. Your data lives on your device, and you can export or import a backup file whenever you like.

Can I install it on my phone?

Yes. Open BurnSet in your mobile browser and choose "Add to Home Screen". It launches full-screen like a native app and works offline.

Completely. No ads, no subscriptions, no paywalled features.
